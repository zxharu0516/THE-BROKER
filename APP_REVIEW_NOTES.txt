THE BROKER — App Review Notes (PUBLIC EDITION v6.0)

• Category / nature: Educational simulation game.
• The app does NOT provide real securities trading, brokerage, investment advisory, portfolio management, account connection, deposits/withdrawals, or purchase links.
• All companies, security identifiers, fundamentals, prices, dividends, drawdowns, and portfolio results shown in gameplay are fictional/game-model data. No real company logos or real stock codes are used.
• The customer personas are fictional and the user is not asked to enter their own real financial circumstances.
• “Low suitability” is a game mechanic measuring fit with a fictional customer profile; it is not a rating or recommendation regarding any real issuer or security.
• Historical macro context is general educational summary only.
• No account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, tracking SDK, or external data transmission is implemented in the current build. Game state and audio preferences are stored locally on device.
• A first-launch disclosure and an in-app Legal & Privacy screen explain these points.

Suggested App Store description lead:
“架空の顧客と架空企業を使い、顧客適合性・分散・リスク管理を学ぶ教育シミュレーションゲーム。実取引・投資助言機能はありません。”
